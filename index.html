Tomás Mezzabarba

Sound Engineer | Digital Artist

Hi! I'm Tomás,

I am an advanced Sound Engineering student focused on acoustics, electroacoustic systems, digital signal processing and audio-oriented software development.

I currently work in production and development at a MIDI controller company, where I am involved in technical processes related to hardware, product assembly, testing and audio/MIDI-oriented workflows. In parallel, I am developing my final degree thesis on broadband acoustic metadiffusers, combining parametric design, acoustic modeling, simulation and experimental validation.

My work connects sound engineering, software tools, measurements, analog audio electronics and 3D design. I am especially interested in projects where acoustics, technology and practical implementation meet.
